# Article Catalog — dinding.partisi.co.id

## Scope

This catalog expands the non-geographic knowledge architecture for operable, folding, and sliding partition-wall systems. It supports Indonesian owners, designers, contractors, buyers, operators, and facility teams from space planning through specification, procurement, installation, operation, maintenance, retrofit, and retirement.

Existing `/standart`, `/semi-peredam`, `/full-peredam`, and `/custom` routes retain commercial inquiry intent. Neutral articles define measurable requirements and evidence; they do not repeat sales copy. The 102 existing city-swapped pages are excluded from editorial expansion and require separate consolidation review.

Catalog statistics: **18 topics and 108 briefs, exactly 6 per topic**. Titles, IDs, slugs, and primary jobs are unique within this domain. Recheck slugs against the live deployment before publication.

Priority legend: **P0** foundation or high-risk decision; **P1** important workflow; **P2** specialist depth or diagnosis; **P3** later-stage durable reference. **W1** foundation, **W2** selection and design, **W3** delivery and assurance, **W4** operation and lifecycle.
