# Topical Authority — dinding.partisi.co.id

## Role and boundary

`dinding.partisi.co.id` should become an Indonesian reference and decision-support hub for operable, folding, and sliding partition-wall systems used to divide interior space. It serves owners, architects, interior designers, acoustic consultants, structural engineers, contractors, estimators, facility teams, operators, and procurement staff from initial space-planning through operation, maintenance, retrofit, and retirement.

The current commercial taxonomy—standard, semi-peredam, full-peredam, and custom—is useful only when each label is converted into a measurable project specification. The knowledge program must distinguish sound insulation from sound absorption, laboratory ratings from installed field performance, panel construction from whole-system performance, and movable partitions from ordinary hinged folding doors or permanent drywall.

Project-specific structural support, fire and egress compliance, accessibility, acoustic acceptance, lifting, electrical integration, and installation authorization remain with competent professionals and current applicable documents. The domain may explain how to ask and verify, but must not invent universal dimensions, loads, decibel values, fire ratings, or code approvals.

Cannibalization is controlled within this domain only. Other Syamsul-owned partition, interior, acoustic, construction, or material domains may independently cover overlapping subjects from their own useful perspective.

## Internal-link architecture

- `/` introduces the commercial systems and links to the PAR-01 knowledge hub without absorbing its neutral educational intent.
- PAR-01 routes readers to use-case planning (PAR-02), system types (PAR-03), anatomy (PAR-04), materials (PAR-05), and acoustic evidence (PAR-06).
- The design path is PAR-02 requirements → PAR-03 system selection → PAR-06 performance → PAR-07 structure → PAR-08 geometry → PAR-09 compliance → PAR-10 documents.
- The delivery path is PAR-10 specification → PAR-11 procurement → PAR-12 factory QA → PAR-13 installation → PAR-14 commissioning.
- The operating path is PAR-14 handover → PAR-15 operation → PAR-16 maintenance and repair → PAR-18 upgrade or end-of-life, with PAR-17 safety linked at every physical-work stage.
- Product routes link to the specific neutral comparison or evidence article that explains their measurable difference. They do not reuse one generic “peredam” guide across all tiers.
- Diagnostic pages link to immediate safe controls, preventive maintenance, repair evidence, and replacement decisions; city pages are never default related links.
- Every article links to its parent hub; hubs list all children; `ARTICLE_CATALOG.md` related IDs establish the initial lateral graph.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Acoustic claims must identify metric, test method and edition, laboratory or field status, specimen configuration, seals, opening and perimeter conditions, frequency treatment, report source, and applicability to the sold system.
- Never translate “peredam” automatically into soundproof. State whether the evidence concerns absorption inside a panel, transmission through a tested assembly, speech privacy, reverberation, or subjective comfort.
- Structural, fire, egress and accessibility pages require current primary sources and competent professional review. Do not publish universal track loads, deflection limits, pass-door dimensions, fire ratings or accessible-force values.
- Product and supplier claims must be supported by current drawings, material records, manufacturer data, samples, test reports, installation records, warranty terms, and service evidence.
- Use original diagrams and project photographs only with permission. Never invent projects, tests, reviews, client counts, prices, ratings, warranty outcomes or firsthand experience.
- Every safety procedure includes authorization, prerequisites, hazards, stop conditions, verification, and emergency escalation.
- Date standards-sensitive pages and maintain an evidence register containing edition, source, access date, reviewer, affected articles, and next review date.
